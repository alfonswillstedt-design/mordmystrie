# THORNMERE HALL — Host Guide

Somebody has to print this and lay it out. That person still plays, still has a character, still has a secret, and does not know who did it. There is no game master here.

**There is a second document called the Solution. Do not read it.** Print it without looking at it, fold it, and seal it inside envelope four. Everything you need in order to run the evening is in this guide.

Read this guide to the end before you print anything.

---

## What you need

- A printer and about 40 sheets of paper
- Scissors
- Four envelopes, ordinary letter size
- Two small envelopes for private notes
- A pen for every player
- A room with a table
- Two to two and a half hours

**Optional but worth it:** low lighting, a rain playlist, one candle.

---

## Printing

| Document | Copies | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Character booklets | 1 of each, 8 total | Each player gets only their own |
| Evidence cards 1–22 | 1 set | Cut apart, keep sorted by round |
| Private notes P1, P2 | 1 each | Into the small envelopes |
| Envelope texts 1–3 | 1 each | Into envelopes one, two and three |
| Envelope labels | 1 sheet | Glue or tape to the envelopes |
| Place cards | 1 sheet | Fold along the line |
| Verdict cards | 1 per player | 8 for a full table |
| This guide | 1 | Keep it to hand — you will use it all evening |
| Solution | 1 | **Do not read.** Fold and seal into envelope four |

**Six players instead of eight:** leave out Father Gideon Hale and Marguerite Sallow. Print no booklets and no place cards for them.

They are still in the house. They were invited, they attended the séance, and they sat at that table all evening — they are simply not played. Every evidence card stays exactly as it is, including the séance record and the note about the door. Nothing else changes and the solution is unaffected.

---

## Setting up

**1.** Cut the evidence cards apart and sort them into three piles using the round marker in the corner of each card. You do not need to read them and you should not.

**2.** Put the pile marked 1 into envelope one, together with the envelope-one text. Same for two and three.

**3.** Envelope four takes the envelope-four label and the Solution. Fold the Solution without reading it. If you catch a line of it by accident, you have spoiled your own evening and nobody else's — say nothing and carry on.

**4.** The two private notes go into the small envelopes, marked with the player names — Nadia and Theo. Put those inside envelope two.

**5.** Seal all four. Put them face down in the middle of the table in order.

**6.** Put out place cards, pens, and a verdict card at each seat.

---

## Before the night

Send the invitation out first, a week or two ahead. It is written in Ambrose's voice and it sets the evening up before anyone arrives — fill in the bracketed date, time and address and send it as it is.

## Handing out characters

You need to decide who plays whom before people arrive. Anyone can do this — knowing who plays which part tells you nothing about who did it.

Send each player their booklet in advance if you can. An hour beforehand is enough; a day is better. Ask them to read it and not discuss it.

If people arrive without having read anything, allow fifteen minutes at the start.

**Nobody is told they are the murderer, because nobody knows.** Not you, not them. The evidence decides it and envelope four confirms it.

---

## The two rules

Say these out loud before you begin.

**One.** You may lie about anything except the contents of an evidence card. Your history, your feelings, where you were, what you were doing — all of it is yours to conceal or misrepresent. What is printed on a card is fact and cannot be denied.

**Two.** Follow the instructions in your booklet exactly, including the ones telling you to admit something at a particular point. They exist so the evening works.

---

## Running the evening

Total: about two hours. Keep an eye on the clock — the rounds run long if nobody minds them.

### Opening — 15 minutes
Everyone reads their booklet. Then go round the table: name, who you are to this family, and one sentence about why you came tonight.

### Round one — 25 minutes
Open envelope one. Read the text aloud. Deal the seven cards and read each one out.

Then question each other freely. Move around. Talk in pairs. Come back.

**Close the round properly:** each player says who they suspect and why. Do not skip this. It is what makes quiet players participate.

### Round two — 30 minutes
Open envelope two. Read the text. Deal the eight cards and read them out. Hand the two small envelopes to Nadia and Theo — they read silently and decide for themselves what to do.

This round is about time. Push people on exact minutes.

Close the same way.

### Round three — 30 minutes
Open envelope three. Read the text. Deal the seven cards.

Go back over what was said earlier. Somebody's account no longer fits.

### Verdict — 20 minutes
Everyone completes a verdict card in silence: who, why, and how you know.

Read them out loud, one at a time.

Then open envelope four and read it in full, including the walkthrough.

---

## If something goes wrong

**A round is running over.** Give a two-minute warning and move on. The evidence in the next envelope is more valuable than more talking about the last one.

**Someone won't engage.** Give them a job — hand them the evidence cards and ask them to keep a list of who claimed to be where.

**Someone works it out in round two.** Ask them to keep it to themselves and to try to prove it rather than announce it. There is a difference between having a hunch about somebody and being able to show why.

**A player refuses to answer anything.** That is Theo, and it is written into his part.

**Nobody has any idea by the verdict.** Perfectly normal. Read the walkthrough — the evening lands either way.

**Somebody guesses right in the first ten minutes.** It happens. Ask them what evidence they have. They won't have any yet.

---

## A note before you start

Nobody at the table knows the answer, including you.

You will suspect the wrong person. So will everyone else. That is the design, and it is why this works with the host playing.

Print it, cut it, seal the envelopes, and find out with everybody else.
